# Interações: imagens e prompts

Imagens criadas com a ferramenta integrada **image_gen**, com fundo transparente. Não foi usada CLI nem API externa. Os recortes são feitos no jogo, sem substituir os sprites de caminhada ou os retratos existentes.

## Ficheiros finais

- `characters/open_world/interaction-poses-v2.png`: Te com a mão no ombro, Te sentado, Jo sentado e Te a apanhar um objeto; consumido por `scripts/interaction_art.gd`.
- `items/forest-items.png`: pedrinha, pinha, ramo, fragmento de cerâmica, pedra com líquenes e molho de ramos; consumido por `scripts/item_art.gd`, tanto no chão como nas bolsas.
- `world/two-seat-bench.png`: banco largo, virado para a frente, com dois lugares; substitui o recorte estreito e diagonal do atlas de objetos na zona aberta.

As novas poses usam os atlas `te-walk-v2.png` e `jo-walk-v2.png` como referências de identidade e estilo. A versão inicial das poses foi descartada após revisão da escala da cabeça e do corpo. A segunda versão foi verificada dentro do jogo, incluindo o ponto de contacto da mão com o ombro e a altura dos bancos. A opacidade original foi preservada.

A calibração comum está em `scripts/world_scale.gd`. O banco foi criado com a ferramenta integrada image_gen e verificado com os dois personagens realmente sentados, com lugares e colisões separados, em vez de aumentar o recorte antigo de um banco individual.

## Prompt final do banco de dois lugares

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE isolated empty wooden park bench sprite for this painterly Sintra semi-top-down game, true transparent alpha background. Image 1 is a MATERIAL/STYLE reference only, image 2 gives the overhead oblique camera angle and character style. The bench must comfortably fit TWO ADULTS next to each other. Wide 2.1-metre seat, normal adult furniture proportions, total width about 2.6 times total projected image height. Bench faces SOUTH toward viewer: front seat edge and backrest are horizontal, symmetrical, NO diagonal rotation in plan, camera looks down at 50 degrees as in game sprites. Rustic weathered timber slats, two modest side armrests, four sturdy short legs, soft moss on ends and lichen on wood, muted warm browns and forest greens. Clear unobstructed SEAT SURFACE with room for two adults, NO plants covering the seating positions or extending widely beyond bench. Clean full silhouette, generous margins, no people, no ground or cast shadows, no text, no scenery. Painterly gouache detail matching references, not a photo, not pixel art. Correct human furniture scale: seat approximately 45cm high, backrest approximately 85cm high; bench is distinctly wide and fairly low, never a toy or a tall chair. Entire bench including feet visible; genuinely transparent background.

All four sprites are isolated cutouts on REAL transparent alpha, no background colour. Keep head/body scale consistent across all four poses; generous empty margins prevent any clipping of arms, hair or shoes.

## Prompt final das poses: correção de proporções

> Use case: identity-preserve. Image 1 is an EDIT TARGET: a transparent four-pose game atlas (Te arm out, Te seated, Jo seated, Te crouching). Images 2 and 3 are REFERENCE SPRITE ATLASES for identity, body/head proportions and overhead camera. Correct image 1 to match these existing game characters much more closely. In particular Te in image 1 currently has too small a head, too narrow a torso and curly hair: give Te his reference short straight tousled dark hair, broad natural charcoal-shirted torso and a noticeably larger head/face matching image 2's semi-top-down game proportions. Jo seated must match image 3's head size and appearance. The camera must look down at 50 degrees as in reference sprites, not a frontal low camera. Match head scale as a fraction of standing body height to the references. Keep exact FOUR poses, 2x2 layout, relative cell positions, arm reaching toward RIGHT of the image at shoulder height in top-left, full figures including shoes and hand, transparent alpha and empty margins. Top-left Te and top-right seated Te and bottom-right crouching Te wear charcoal tee, light grey shorts and black shoes. Bottom-left Jo wears burgundy tee, beige shorts and pale shoes. Do not add furniture, shadows, words, scenery or another person. Preserve genuine transparency. Natural adult anatomy and painterly art identical to references. Correct character proportions and identity ONLY.

Each object wholly contained in its own equal cell, generous TRANSPARENT gutters and margins. No other objects, no environment, no cast ground shadows, no text, no labels, no border, no grid. Clean readable silhouettes, rich painted material detail, true alpha transparent background. This is a sprite sheet used both as objects on the ground and as small inventory icons.
