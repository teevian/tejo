# Serra de Sintra — um caminho lembrado

Gerado com a ferramenta de imagem integrada, a partir da direção artística e das fotografias de referência fornecidas na conversa. O resultado é uma pintura de fundo; a ponte e os degraus pertencem ao cenário, e a faixa horizontal de terra coincide com o chão jogável existente em y=554.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: finished original background painting for a 2D side-scrolling indie game, 16:9 landscape, ideally 2560 x 1440. Full bleed opaque artwork, no UI or characters.
Primary request: a small intimate stretch of the Serra de Sintra forest in Portugal, inspired by the user's trail photographs: tall eucalyptus with peeling pale bark, slim crooked trees, ivy carpeting damp banks, moss-covered granite boulders, sword-shaped leaves and ferns, leaf litter, rough log steps and a tiny handmade log footbridge.
Style/medium: invent a cohesive personal painterly voice, 'a landscape remembered rather than recorded'. Matte gouache mixed with charcoal and dry pastel on softly grained paper; confident broken brush marks, uneven scraped pigment, unfinished soft silhouettes receding into mist; some near details crisp and tactile, others dissolve. Subtle adventurous fine-art indie aesthetic, slightly strange elongated trunks, sensitive irregular silhouettes, deep aubergine shadows beneath restrained moss green, petrol teal and grey sage. Sparse warm ochre and pale butter light. Avoid generic glossy fantasy concept art.
Composition/framing: SIDE-ON 2D GAME STAGE, a wide continuous nearly level earth trail crosses left to right. In normalized image coordinates the playable foot line is exactly y=0.77 (554 of 720) across the FULL width, so actors can walk without looking suspended. The earth trail is a horizontal sandy-ochre ribbon around y=0.76-0.85, softly littered with eucalyptus leaves, no rocks or solid obstacles in that playable ribbon. Near foliage below y=0.90. Keep the central stage around x=0.35-0.65, y=0.60-0.77 quiet, clear and slightly luminous for two small playable characters. Behind that side-on playable trail, the ground slopes down into an intimate wooded hollow, with a tiny rustic log footbridge over a dark shallow rill at x around 0.28, y around 0.64, and a narrow log-stepped trail drifting uphill and disappearing into pale mist at the center-left. Tall trees form layered vertical rhythms and an asymmetric canopy; a diagonal mossy fallen trunk in the back at the right. Do NOT render the primary playable trail as a first-person road receding from the bottom center. Side-scrolling composition first, the smaller distant path can recede. Dark shapes frame the far edges without blocking the stage. Upper left has muted light mist behind trunks, enough calm for a small overlay title. Layered depth, air between trees, visual breathing space.
Lighting/mood: a cool humid Sintra morning with a few dusty pale-gold flecks of sun, quiet tenderness, solitude, lived-in familiarity, restrained wonder; no bright magic or oversaturated glow.
Constraints: only forest and paths, no humans, no creatures, no buildings, no lettering, no text, no logos, no border, no watermark, no interface, no map symbols, no photorealism, no vector-art look, no 3D look. A finished hand-painted game background.
