# Open-world Sintra asset

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original portraits and painted sprites are the identity/style reference; the camera changes to semi-top-down.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: transparent game scenery sprite atlas, 3 columns by 2 rows, six equal cells with generous transparent padding and NO overlap. Same hand-painted gouache Serra de Sintra forest style as reference, but viewed at a fixed semi-top-down oblique RPG angle 55 degrees, orthographic, see tops of rocks and tree canopy. Row1 cell1: entire slender tall eucalyptus with hanging sage green leaves and peeling pale grey bark; cell2: entire broad mature cork oak with irregular dark olive leafy crown and thick reddish textured trunk; cell3: entire tree fern with elegant spreading fronds and short textured trunk. Row2 cell1: cluster of rounded moss-covered grey granite boulders; cell2: a small old low stone spring/fountain wall and basin, seen from above/front, no water beyond basin; cell3: one weathered wooden trail bench with a small patch of ivy at feet. FULL objects must fit entirely within their own cell; transparent gutters, no cell borders, no labels. All objects stand upright on invisible ground, base near bottom of own cell. True alpha transparent background, no scenery behind objects, subtle ground contact shadows only. Natural sophisticated painterly style with broken brush strokes, muted warm greens, ochre lights, lavender cool shadows. Not pixel art, not flat icons, no text. Atlas 3:2.
