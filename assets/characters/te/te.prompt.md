# Te — retrato e poses pintadas

Criado com a ferramenta de imagem integrada a partir das fotografias de Te fornecidas pelo utilizador. `te-portrait.png` define as feições e aparece no avatar. `te-sprites.png` contém doze poses completas: quatro repousos, seis passos, subida e descida do salto. As cabeças estão pintadas nos ângulos das próprias poses, com base no retrato e nas fotografias de perfil. Roupa: t-shirt preta/cinzento muito escuro, calções cinzentos claros e ténis pretos.

As imagens de Jo são usadas somente como referência do estilo de pintura e da organização das poses. Os recursos e o desenho de Jo foram preservados.

## Retrato

Use case: identity-preserve / stylized-concept.
Asset type: transparent painted head-and-shoulders portrait for Te, a new playable character in the same intimate indie game as Jo.
Reference roles: the first four images are Te's identity references. Screenshot 00.56.54 is the primary clear frontal face reference; 00.58.55 supplies the gentle expression and eyes; 00.58.29 supplies his actual side-profile anatomy; 00.57.52 supplies cheek, brow and lip relationships. The LAST image (jo-portrait.png) is ONLY the game's paint/style reference. Do not copy Jo's facial geometry, abundant wavy hair, hooked nose or toothy grin.
Request: portray the SAME MAN from the Te photographs faithfully in this original hand-painted style. Preserve the recognizable dark brown expressive almond eyes and natural slightly heavy upper lids, strong dark fairly horizontal subtly arched eyebrows, fuller oval face and softly square/rounded jaw and chin, medium-long defined nose with a nearly straight/slightly convex bridge and natural rounded tip as seen in the side photo, ears, and the particular thin upper lip and gently pressed asymmetric closed-lip smile. He has short almost-black brown hair, softly tousled but flatter and less voluminous than Jo's, with wispy irregular strands falling forward on the forehead and short natural temples. Warm light olive skin; very light beard shadow on jaw/chin and a fine short moustache, as in the main clear portrait, not a full beard. Keep his familiar quiet, kind, slightly thoughtful expression and adult facial proportions, no big cartoon eyes or exaggerated heroic jaw.
Wardrobe: plain BLACK / very dark charcoal short-sleeve crew-neck t-shirt, visible upper shoulders; no logos, jacket, backpack, glasses, jewellery or earbuds.
View: a gentle nearly frontal three-quarter view, 10-15 degrees turned toward the viewer's right, with both eyes readable. Whole hair, ears, face and upper chest visible, centered and comfortably framed.
Style: exactly the same intimate matte gouache as the Jo portrait: textured broken brush strokes, subtle charcoal/pastel accents, restrained warm cream light, olive-grey shadow, softly irregular painterly edges, human asymmetry and tenderness. Clearly painted with believable anatomy; no photorealistic cutout, vector, shiny 3D, chibi or generic cartoon redesign.
Composition: one portrait on an actual transparent background, square, comfortably filling the canvas with transparent margins around hair and shoulders. No landscape, floor, text, labels, frames, watermark or checkerboard. Ignore background people, interfaces and lettering in the photos.
