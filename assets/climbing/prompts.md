# Prompts finais

Ferramenta: image_gen integrada. Ambos são novos assets; as fotografias do utilizador orientaram o tema e os materiais.

## Panorama

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: painted parallax background for an existing 2D cooperative rock-climbing game set in Serra de Sintra, Portugal. Create a wide landscape matte painting, 1536x1024 or larger, in detailed hand-painted gouache/oil storybook adventure-game style, natural proportions, mature warm quiet atmosphere, no outlines. View from midway up a sheer granite mountain: the left two thirds opens onto a vast densely forested green valley, tiny white Portuguese villages and terracotta roofs far below, Atlantic blue haze on horizon, pale blue sky and wisps of cloud. On far right, giant weathered pale silver grey granite cliffs with broad rounded boulders, dark cracks, lichen and moss, coastal scrub. In upper right distance sits the crenellated grey stone rampart of Castelo dos Mouros. Inspired by user's Sintra reference photographs: immense rounded granite outcrops, lichen speckles, deep green pine and broadleaf woodland, sunlit cream stone, distant ocean. Warm afternoon side light, atmospheric depth, rich brushwork. This is distant scenery behind independent playable rock platforms. Keep middle fairly open for characters and platforms. No people, no climbers, no ropes, no text, no lettering, no UI, no border, no watermark.

## Atlas de granito

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent sprite atlas for a 2D side-view rock-climbing adventure in Sintra. Create one 1536x1024 image with EXACTLY six separate large granite boulder sprites in a clean 3-column by 2-row grid, every object fully contained in its cell and separated by generous TRANSPARENT space. Truly transparent background. All six seen directly from the side, matching same lighting and scale, with a mostly horizontal walkable top edge at approximately same height in their cells, irregular rounded undersides, weighty three-dimensional stone mass. Top row: (1) broad low rounded pale silver granite shelf with moss at left; (2) tall split cream-grey granite boulder with vertical dark crack and lichen; (3) long narrow balanced granite slab resting on small round fulcrum, moss on edges. Bottom row: (4) wide rounded massive Sintra summit rock with speckled lichen and a clean flat upper ridge; (5) small loose round granite stone, no pedestal; (6) rugged tall sunlit granite tower with fractured edge. Detailed painted gouache/oil storybook game art, natural and tactile brushwork, late afternoon sun from upper left, pale cool grey and warm cream surfaces, dark fissures, subtle sage lichen, tiny ferns and heather on a few edges. The stones must look like real granite penedos from Serra de Sintra. No characters, no ropes, no text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no ground scene, no rectangular backgrounds, no shadows detached from rocks, no watermark.
