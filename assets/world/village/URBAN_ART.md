## Pedido final — fachadas urbanas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D game environment sprite atlas for the Tejo Godot village.
Primary request: Eight different TALL attached urban Portuguese Sintra street-front buildings, inspired by the user's photographs of the historic centre, painted to match the warm detailed gouache game houses in the supporting style reference. NEW ORIGINAL art, not photos.
Composition: a perfectly separated FOUR COLUMN by TWO ROW atlas on a transparent canvas, each building entirely within its equal cell with 24px transparent gutter. 3072x2048 landscape. One building per cell, same orthographic elevated front view, parallel verticals, only a slim right side visible, roofs visible from above, bottom front doorstep at same baseline in each row. NO ground or trees behind. No perspectives converging toward a vanishing point. They are narrow party-wall buildings intended to stand shoulder to shoulder in continuous rows. Flat straight side walls, very small eaves; do not attach vines around outer silhouettes. Realistic consistent door scale, three floors in most.
Top row left to right: 1) mustard yellow three-storey square house, cream stone pilasters, white sash windows, small black wrought-iron balconies and burgundy shop awning; 2) weathered pale grey four-storey facade with central ornate stone balcony, white windows, small dark shop doors; 3) pale blue three-storey tall gabled house with terracotta roof, stone trim, iron balconies and dark green cafe storefront; 4) salmon pink three-storey CORNER cafe building, a slim right face visible, tiled low entrance canopy, white window trim, green shutters, empty small sign board.
Bottom row left to right: 5) deep turquoise blue/green azulejo tile three-storey house, tall arched windows and intricate iron balconies, pale limestone frames; 6) warm white plaster three-storey residential building, dark green shutters, slightly weathered base, a few tiny flower pots at windows, no shop; 7) pale butter-yellow two-storey residential house with grey granite frames and a narrow residential door; 8) blue-white azulejo three-storey townhouse, sober tiled geometry, white sash windows and white stone trim, dark navy ground-floor ceramics storefront.
Materials: small terracotta roof tiles, worn lime plaster, intricate black ironwork, Portuguese azulejo patterns. Rich painted details at readable game scale, soft ambient daytime light with subtle shadows confined to architecture. No people, no cars, no detached cottage composition, no vegetation background, no drop shadow outside buildings, no labels, no watermark. True transparent alpha exterior. Keep architecture opaque within the silhouette. Style reference is the attached existing game houses only; proportions should be taller and urban.

## Pedido final — calçada

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless square ground texture for an elevated 2D Portuguese village game.
Make an ORIGINAL hand-painted detailed warm gouache texture of Portuguese calcada pavement as in the user's Sintra alley photographs: very small irregular ivory limestone setts on a charcoal-grey basalt field, forming an orderly repeating grid of ivory DIAMONDS, each diamond 5 small stones wide and 5 high. About 4 complete diamond motifs horizontally and vertically in the tile. A thin dark grout around every tiny sett, stone grey/cream not brown rubble. Flat overhead orthographic view, no perspective, NO scene, no borders, no text, no buildings, no objects, no vegetation, no shadows, no gradient lighting. Square 1536x1536, seamless repeat left/right and top/bottom, consistent texel density and soft natural subtle stone surface variation. Opaque full bleed texture.

## Pedido final — mercado e mobiliário

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent production sprite atlas for a warm painted Sintra 2D adventure game.
Create original detailed painterly gouache street market objects, based on the user's photographs of ceramics, souvenir shops, potted stair alleys and cafe terraces. EXACTLY four columns and three rows of separate objects on transparent canvas, equal cells, each fully isolated with a generous clear gutter; 2048x1536 landscape. Elevated orthographic frontal 2.5D view, verticals parallel, warm muted authentic Portuguese materials. Each object on its own, no scenic background, NO people.
Top row: 1) small cream canvas awning on dark green posts sheltering a wooden produce stall with crates of oranges and tomatoes; 2) blue and cream striped canvas street stall filled with ceramic jugs, plates and blue-white azulejo panels; 3) wooden souvenir display with patterned linen bags, folded textiles and a tall postcard rack; 4) round off-white parasol over a cafe table with two delicate green iron chairs.
Middle row: 5) terracotta pot with lush hydrangea pink flowers; 6) a row of three flower boxes with red geraniums; 7) long cream carved stone balustrade viewed across front with 8 balusters and pillars, straight horizontal top; 8) weathered Sintra stone pillory column with a detailed old carved capital on three concentric octagonal steps, slender and tall, no lettering.
Bottom row: 9) large round carved limestone historic Portuguese fountain, central narrow multi-level column, scalloped basin, subtle pale green water, matching Sintra main square photographs; 10) wooden street chalkboard on A-frame with tiny abstract chalk strokes no readable words; 11) shallow wicker baskets with colorful ceramic plates and rolled textiles outside shop; 12) curved wrought-iron bracket with a traditional black square hanging lantern, ornate curl.
Rendering: tactile brush painted stone, terracotta and wood, gentle ambient daylight, architecture-scale game art. No grass or ground slab beneath objects except fountain/pillory bases. True transparent alpha outside silhouettes and in gaps of ironwork and balustrade. Do not add names, captions, watermarks or grid lines.

## Pavimentação orientada — revisão

A textura de pedra existente fornece o grão ao shader `shaders/village_paving.gdshader`. Os fiados são construídos no motor, com juntas gastas e variação discreta entre pedras; o granito central usa coordenadas que seguem o comprimento e a largura do percurso. A calçada clara envolve as fachadas e o bordo do passeio; os losangos do atlas ficam só em dois painéis das praças. Os novos aglomerados reutilizam as pinturas aprovadas de fachadas frontais e laterais. Não houve geração de novas imagens nesta revisão.
